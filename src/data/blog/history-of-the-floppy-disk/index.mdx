Before the cloud, before USB, an 8-inch magnetic square held the entire state of a machine — and
taught a generation what "saving" meant.

## From 8-Inch to 3.5

The floppy shrank from 8 inches, to 5.25, to the rigid 3.5-inch shell that outlived them all.
Capacity crept from a few hundred kilobytes to a "high density" 1.44 MB — a figure burned into the
memory of anyone who ever shipped software in a cardboard box.

Its greatest legacy is a glyph: the save icon. Millions of people tap a picture of a device they have
never physically held. The medium is dead; its silhouette is immortal.
